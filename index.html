About Elections & Voting

Elections are the cornerstone of democratic governance:

  • Representation: Voters select leaders who represent their interests.
  • Accountability: Regular elections keep leaders answerable to the public.
  • Legitimacy: Free and fair elections give government its authority.
  • Choice: Voters decide between policies, parties, and candidates.
  • Participation: Voting is direct civic engagement in democracy.

Your vote matters and shapes the future of your community and nation.
